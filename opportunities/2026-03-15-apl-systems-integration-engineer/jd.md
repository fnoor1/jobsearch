# Systems Integration Engineer — APL (Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory)

## Overview

Member of an AI/ML prototyping team developing capabilities for national security missions. Responsible for deploying hardware, software, and sensor systems to prototype and deliver cutting-edge intelligent systems.

## Responsibilities

- Work with an experienced, highly-capable AI/ML development team to solve current national security problems.
- Help design and maintain lab computer systems in support of AI/ML prototyping activities.
- Master the configuration, operation, and troubleshooting of sophisticated information systems.
- Take a hands-on role in system configuration to represent real-world platform configurations and maintain knowledge of fielded configurations to support remote troubleshooting.
- Support installation, field testing, troubleshooting, and training at various sponsor field sites and military facilities.

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Four years of technical professional experience installing computer hardware and software systems to support industrial or national security applications, including operating system and network configuration.
- Possess or acquire within six months of employment CompTIA A+, CompTIA Network+, and CompTIA Security+ or comparable certifications and comply with requirements for privileged users of information systems.
- Experience working with system users to ensure that needs are met and continuity of operations is maintained.
- Passionate about working in a high-paced environment supporting the real-world operational needs of a wide variety of end-users.
- Excited to engage with, teach, and learn from others.
- Able to obtain an Interim Secret level security clearance by start date and can ultimately obtain Secret level clearance. U.S. citizenship required.

## Preferred Qualifications

- MS degree or higher in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Experience configuring Linux and Windows, including shell or PowerShell scripting and networking.
- Knowledge of configuring and administering SQL database systems.
- Working knowledge of ubiquitous programming languages, e.g., C/C++, Java, Python.
- Active Secret or higher clearance.

## Special Working Conditions

- Some work outside normal working hours and on weekends for operations and maintenance might be rarely required.
- Occasional travel and work at government facilities including underway on aircraft and ships, requiring work outside of and in excess of a typical work week.
- Must be able to lift a minimum of 50 pounds to facilitate the moving and installation of hardware.
